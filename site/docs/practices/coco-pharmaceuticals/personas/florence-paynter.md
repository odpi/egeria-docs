# Florence Paynter - Graduate Chemist


----

![Icon](florence-paynter.png)

----

??? info "Who is Florence Paynter?"
    ## Persona Description

    **Age**: 24
    
    **Skills**: Chemistry (first class degree).  No experience of manufacturing yet.
    
    **Scope**: Manufacturing Plant.
    
    **Job**:
    
    * Florence is a recent graduate who has just joined [Stew Faster](/practices/coco-pharmaceuticals/personas/stew-faster)'s manufacturing team.
    * She is working closely with Stew to learn as much as she can about how Coco Pharmaceuticals makes its products.
    
    **Thinks/Feels**:
    
    * Florence is very bright and keen, and enjoying the chance to apply her chemistry in a real business.
    * She is conscious of how much she does not yet know about manufacturing, and is anxious not to slow the team down.
    
    **Hears**:
    
    * Stew explaining how the manufacturing lines work.  He rarely has to tell her anything twice.
    * Occasional frustration from Stew when she does not understand something that he thinks is basic.
    
    **Says/Does**:
    
    * Asks a lot of questions, and takes careful notes so that she does not need to ask them again.
    * Looks for the reasons behind each step of the manufacturing process, not just the steps themselves.
    
    **Sees**:
    
    * A great deal of knowledge about the manufacturing process that is held in the heads of experienced people, such as Stew, rather than written down where a newcomer can find it.
    
    **Top challenges**:
    
    * Getting up to speed with manufacturing quickly, without the background that the rest of the team takes for granted.
    * Knowing which of her questions are basic, and where to find the answers for herself.
    
    **Desired Outcome**:
    
    * To become a productive member of the manufacturing team as quickly as possible.
    * To build a career in which she can make use of her chemistry.
