![Icon](george-pie.png)

??? info "Who is George Pie?"
    ## Persona Description

    **Age**: 28
    
    **Skills**: Manufacturing engineering, with 5 years of experience in the manufacturing team at Austin.
    
    **Scope**: Coco Pharmaceuticals' manufacturing sites.
    
    **Job**:
    
    * George was originally employed by the small-batch manufacturer in Austin that Coco Pharmaceuticals recently acquired.  He is on secondment to [Stew Faster](/practices/coco-pharmaceuticals/personas/stew-faster)'s manufacturing team.
    * His role is to help Stew adapt the core Coco Pharmaceuticals manufacturing sites to more flexible ways of working.
    
    **Thinks/Feels**:
    
    * George has only ever known the way that Austin works, and he is surprised by how differently the original Coco Pharmaceuticals manufacturing sites operate - and by how different again the recently acquired Bucharest site is.
    
    **Hears**:
    
    * Explanations from the original Coco Pharmaceuticals teams of why their processes are the way they are.
    
    **Says/Does**:
    
    * Shares how Austin runs its small-batch manufacturing, and works with Stew to identify which of those practices could be adopted at the other sites.
    
    **Sees**:
    
    * Three sets of manufacturing practices - Austin, Bucharest and the original Coco Pharmaceuticals sites - that each work for the site that uses them, but differ widely from one another.
    
    **Top challenges**:
    
    * Understanding why each site works the way it does before recommending changes.
    * Introducing more flexible ways of working without disrupting the high standards of quality and consistency that the original sites are known for.
    
    **Desired Outcome**:
    
    * Manufacturing sites that can respond flexibly to the move to personalized medicine, while keeping the strengths of each.
